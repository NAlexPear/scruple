With gpt-6-luna, input costs $0.10 per 1M tokens. You pay only for input tokens: there are no cache-read, cache-write, or output-token charges.

Regional processing premiums and long-context input pricing multipliers apply. This text workload did not use long context. Any account-specific regional premium was not observable from benchmark responses and is excluded from the estimate.
